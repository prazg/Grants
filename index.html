Institutional Strategy

NAAMII & Nepal Grant Eligibility Playbook

Nepal holds overlapping international classifications that unlock distinct funding streams when framed strategically.

EU & Bilateral

1. Automatic LMIC Funding in Horizon Europe & SNSF

Under European Commission rules, legal entities established in Nepal are automatically eligible for direct EC funding in Horizon Europe (Cluster 1 Health & Cluster 4 Digital/AI). Combined with NAAMII's existing EU grant execution track record (UDAAN), European coordinators can add NAAMII as a funded South Asian validation & AI partner at zero extra national co-financing cost. Similarly, Switzerland's SNSF SPIRIT funds direct salaries and equipment in Nepal.

Global Health AI

2. Leveraging 2026–2027 South Asia & Evaluation Shifts

Major global health donors have pivoted from exploratory AI pilots to locally led clinical evaluation (e.g., the $60M EVAH Initiative and IDRC AI4GH), while the UK's NIHR Global Health Research explicitly narrowed its geographic focus to South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa. Anchor proposals in NAAMII's WHO SEARO collaboration, NHRC ethical review, and Nepali teaching hospitals.

AI Infrastructure

3. Stacking Compute, Datasets & STLC Hardware Grants

Standalone GPU hardware grants are scarce globally, but Nepal is classified by UNESCO/TWAS as a priority Science & Technology Lagging Country (STLC). Stack TWAS Research Unit Grants (up to $54K for physical hardware) and OWSD Fellowships ($50K incl. equipment) with NVIDIA Academic GPU Grants, Lacuna Fund dataset calls, and rolling AWS / GCP / Azure cloud credits.

Proposal Readiness

4. Institutional Registrations Checklist for PIs

Many international calls have fast 30–60 day turnarounds. Keep institutional identifiers active: EU PIC (Horizon Europe), SAM.gov UEI & NIH eRA Commons (Fogarty K43/R21), ORCID & Fluxx (Grand Challenges), ADB CMS (Digital Health TA), and 501(c)(3) Equivalency Determination (NGOSource) for US philanthropies (Gates, Mozilla, CZI, McGovern).
